KLI ACCESS INFRASTRUCTURE™ // KLAT

Verification precedes access. Contribution precedes allocation.

KLAT™ (Kelly Legacy Access Token™) functions as a governance participation and contribution-recognition infrastructure within the Kelly Legacy ecosystem. The system is designed around verification, contribution, continuity, governance participation, and institutional access structure.

What KLAT Is
