The Problem

Most organizations do not fail from lack of effort. They fail from lack of structure.

Unclear Procedure

Processes exist informally, but are not sequenced, documented, or repeatable.

Weak Records

Activity occurs without a durable institutional record, making review and accountability difficult.

Role Confusion

Individuals act without clear capacity, authority, responsibility, or administrative boundaries.

Continuity Risk

Knowledge is dependent on people instead of preserved through systems, records, and governance infrastructure.
