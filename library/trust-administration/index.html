Trust Administration

A structured knowledge collection covering fiduciary authority, trustee responsibilities, trust property management, beneficiary protection, and equitable enforcement.

Trust administration is the disciplined management of entrusted property according to fiduciary obligations, governing instruments, beneficiary interests, and recognized principles of equity.

Effective administration requires:

  • clear authority
  • proper capacity
  • accurate records
  • preservation of property
  • beneficiary accountability
  • fiduciary compliance

Trust governance operates through structure, not assumption.
