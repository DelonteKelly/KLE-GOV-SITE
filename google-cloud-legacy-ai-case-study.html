Google Cloud / Legacy AI™ Implementation Case Study

A public record documenting how Kelly Legacy Estates is using Google Cloud infrastructure, Agent Platform, Search Console, structured data, and AI governance principles to develop Legacy AI™ as an institutional intelligence system.

Executive Summary

Legacy AI™ is being developed as the institutional intelligence interface for Kelly Legacy Estates, Kelly Legacy Institute, and the Office of the Fiduciary. The implementation is designed to support governance education, fiduciary literacy, curriculum navigation, publication organization, and AI governance awareness while preserving human review and institutional accountability.

Governance principle: AI may support administration, but it must not replace fiduciary judgment, professional review, or formal institutional authority.

Institutional Problem

Educational institutions, founders, and governance teams increasingly use AI tools without documented controls. This creates risks involving misinformation, unmanaged records, unclear authority, data exposure, and lack of human review. Legacy AI™ responds to this issue by placing structure, boundaries, and accountability around AI-supported knowledge systems.

Implementation Objective

The objective is to build a controlled AI knowledge interface that can assist users with approved educational content, institutional navigation, governance frameworks, and structured knowledge retrieval without creating unauthorized legal, financial, fiduciary, or professional advice.

Lessons Learned

The implementation demonstrated that institutional AI requires structure before scale. Public identity, technical discovery, governance language, source boundaries, and human review standards should be established before deploying user-facing AI tools.
